# Capricorn Championship routing

`capricorn-championship.json` contains OpenStreetMap hole ways and green outlines retrieved 9 November 2026 from the OSM map API, bbox `150.740,-23.073,150.771,-23.046`. © OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL: https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright . No satellite imagery or traced Google geometry is included.

The combined boundary way 332993065 describes 36 holes. The western 18-hole routing (way IDs listed in course-layout.ts) is the Championship. The other routing is excluded. Source hole directions are corrected by their endpoint's proximity to the 18 mapped green polygons; five source ways already run tee-to-green. The connected routing, opening par 5, par-4 fourth and par-5 eighteenth agree with the first-person Championship walkthrough at https://aussiegolfquest.com/capricorn-resort-golf-course/ (played December 2016). Pars use that walkthrough, not length thresholds. Lengths remain OSM path measurements, not certified white-tee scorecard distances. Green outlines and hazards may be incomplete or outdated.

Closure source: Golf Operations Manager Andrew Humble's letter, https://capricornenterprise.com.au/wp-content/uploads/2015/10/Golf-members-letter.pdf (Resort closed October 2015; Championship remained open).

The test fixture retains both 18-hole routings to reproduce the importer defect. Mapping revision changes keep a local backup and invalidate old local marker/shot coordinates; scores, clubs and photos remain saved.
