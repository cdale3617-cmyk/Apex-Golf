# APEX Golf

An on-course golf caddie for Android. It works without a server: your bag, rounds, shots and courses are saved on the phone.

What it does
- Find a course by name or town (OpenStreetMap) and load every hole: par, length, front/centre/back of the green, which way it points.
- Today's wind and temperature for the course (Open-Meteo).
- Play one hole at a time with a drawn hole map, a compass, and a metres or yards switch.
- On-device caddie: club call, aim, plays-like distance for wind, slope, heat and lie. It learns your real distances from shots you log.
- Voice: talk to the caddie and hear the answer.
- 3D Photo: turn a fairway photo into an illustrated 3D picture and attach it to a hole.
- Scorecards for every round.

Getting the app on your phone
1. Open the Actions tab and run "Build Android APK" (it also runs on every push to main).
2. When it finishes, open the Releases page and download APEX-Golf.apk on your phone.
3. Open the file and allow "install unknown apps" for your browser when Android asks.

This is a debug build, which is fine for your own phone. Course search, weather and map data need a connection; everything else works offline.
